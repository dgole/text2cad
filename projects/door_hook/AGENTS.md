# Door Hook

## Description

An over-the-door hook that clips onto the top edge of a door and provides a
flat horizontal platform extending into the room. Designed to hold a small
portable projector. The clip is thin enough that the door can still close
with the hook attached.

Cross-section is a U-channel (front wall + bridge + back wall) that slides
down over the door top, with a shelf extending outward from the front wall.
